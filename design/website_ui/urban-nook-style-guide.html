01 — Core Idea

Restraint = Premium

Three colours, four type styles, one red dot. The discipline is the luxury. Every genre lives inside duotone photography and labels — not new brand colours — so anime, auto, tech and beyond all share one confident, editorial identity.

1

Strict three-colour palette

Red, off-white, black on every page. No genre colours in the UI. This is what makes it read as a premium label rather than a busy marketplace.

2

Photography carries the genre

Product and lifestyle shots — often duotone or red-tinted — bring each world to life. The frame stays neutral; the imagery does the talking.

3

The red dot is the signature

A single red circle recurs across pages, packaging and socials — a tiny, ownable mark that ties everything together.
