About Urban Nook

We build the one-stop store for people who treat their desk like a statement.

Urban Nook started with a simple problem: if you love anime and cars and a clean tech setup, you end up buying from ten different stores. We thought — why not one?

So we built a curated home for aesthetic, fandom-driven gear. Posters and katanas for the anime crowd. 3D-printed caliper lamps and keychain holders for the car people. Desk shelves, cable docks, planters and RGB for everyone upgrading their space. New worlds get added all the time.

What we believe

Your space should show your world. A desk isn't just where you work — it's a shelf for the things you love. We obsess over build quality, curation, and a look that's premium without being precious.

The promise

No matter your fandom, your desk can flex it — and you can get it all in one place. Fast dispatch, easy returns, and free shipping over ₹999.
